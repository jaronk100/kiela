KIELA

Four-row mancala of the Kimbundu people, Angola — "the game of peace"
How to play (quick reference)

Board: 4 rows × 10 holes. You control the bottom two rows (your outer row is nearest you, your inner row faces the computer). Seeds travel anticlockwise around your own two rows only: rightward along your outer row, then leftward back along your inner row.

Your turn: click a highlighted hole. Its seeds are sown one per hole anticlockwise. If the last seed lands in an empty hole, your turn ends. If it lands in an occupied hole, everything in that hole is picked up and sowing continues (multi-lap).

Capture: if a lap ends in an occupied hole of your inner row and the computer's inner hole directly opposite is occupied, you capture those seeds — plus the seeds in its outer hole behind it, if occupied. Captured seeds go into your hand and you keep sowing them into your own rows, starting after the hole that made the capture.

Losing: in Advanced rules you lose when you cannot move (every hole empty or holding a single seed — singles cannot be sown). In Beginner rules singles can be sown and you only lose when your side is completely empty.
